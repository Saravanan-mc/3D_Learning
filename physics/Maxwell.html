Technical Reference

∇ (Del Operator): In the equations above, the nabla symbol represents the vector differential operator. When followed by a dot (∇·), it represents Divergence—the "outflow" of a field from a point. When followed by a cross (∇×), it represents Curl—the circulation of the field around a point.

ε₀ & μ₀: These are the fundamental constants of the universe—the permittivity and permeability of free space. Maxwell discovered that the speed of light c is exactly 1 / sqrt(ε₀μ₀), proving light is an electromagnetic wave.

The EM Wave Identity: In the "Propagation" mode, notice how the Electric Field (E) and Magnetic Field (B) are strictly perpendicular to each other and to the direction of travel. This "self-sustaining" cycle is what allows light to travel through the vacuum of space without a medium.

Historical Context: Before Maxwell (1861), electricity and magnetism were seen as separate forces. These equations unified them, paving the way for Einstein's Relativity and the modern digital age.
