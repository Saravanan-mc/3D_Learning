Scope of Physics

The Natural Philosophy

Physics is the fundamental science that attempts to describe how the universe behaves. It involves the study of physical quantities, their measurements, and the relationships between them.

Classical Physics

Mechanics, Thermodynamics, Electromagnetism, and Optics.

Modern Physics

Quantum Mechanics, Relativity, Nuclear and Particle Physics.

Why Physics Matters

Physics is the foundation for all other sciences like Chemistry, Biology, and Astronomy. It drives technological innovation—from the smartphone in your pocket to the rockets exploring Mars.

The Four Forces
  • Gravity: Infinite range, attractive only.
  • Electromagnetism: Attraction/Repulsion of charges.
  • Strong Nuclear: Holds the nucleus together.
  • Weak Nuclear: Responsible for radioactive decay.

Accuracy & Precision

The Difference Matters

Accuracy refers to how close a measurement is to the true or accepted value.

Precision refers to how close measurements of the same item are to each other (consistency).

Types of Errors
  • • Systematic Error: Constant error due to equipment (Affects Accuracy).
  • • Random Error: Variations in readings (Affects Precision).
