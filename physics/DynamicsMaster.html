Newton's Laws of Motion

First Law

Inertia

An object at rest stays at rest, and an object in motion stays in motion unless acted upon by an external force.

Second Law

F = ma

The acceleration of an object depends on the net force acting upon the object and the mass of the object.

Third Law

Action/Reaction

For every action force, there is an equal and opposite reaction force acting on different objects.

Momentum & Collisions

Conservation of Momentum

In a closed system, the total momentum before a collision is equal to the total momentum after.

m₁v₁ + m₂v₂ = m₁v₁' + m₂v₂'

Friction Fundamentals

Static vs. Kinetic Friction

Notice the "Breakaway Point". Static friction ($f_s \le \mu_s N$) is always greater than kinetic friction ($f_k = \mu_k N$). Once the object starts moving, the resistance drops.
Static Friction

Prevents movement. It matches the applied force up to a maximum threshold.

Kinetic Friction

Opposes a sliding object. Generally constant regardless of speed.

Coefficient of Friction (μ)

A dimensionless number that represents the "roughness" between two surfaces.
