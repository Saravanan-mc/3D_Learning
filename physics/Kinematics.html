Distance vs. Displacement

Conceptual Difference

Distance (Scalar)

The total path length covered by an object. It only has magnitude.

Displacement (Vector)

The straight-line distance from the start point to the end point. Includes direction.

3D Projectile Motion

2D Independence

Projectile motion is a combination of horizontal motion with constant velocity and vertical motion with constant acceleration (gravity).

Key Variables
  • Launch Angle: Changes the range and height.
  • Gravity: Constantly pulls down at $9.8 m/s^2$.

Equations of Motion

The Big Three (Constant Acceleration)

v = u + at
Relates velocity, time, and acceleration.
s = ut + ½at²
Relates position, time, and acceleration.
v² = u² + 2as
Independent of time.
