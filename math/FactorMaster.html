The Fundamentals

Factors

Factors are the whole numbers that divide into another number without leaving a remainder. They are the "multiplication partners" that form a specific product.

Multiples

Multiples are the results of multiplying a number by an integer (1, 2, 3...). While factors are limited, multiples are infinite.

Prime vs. Composite

Number Classification

Prime Numbers 2 Factors Only

A prime number has exactly two distinct factors: 1 and itself. It is the "atom" of mathematics.

Composite Numbers 3+ Factors

A composite number has more than two factors. It can be broken down into a product of prime numbers.

1 is neither prime nor composite.
