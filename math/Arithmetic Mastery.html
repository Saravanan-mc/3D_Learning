Fundamental Properties

Commutative

Order doesn't matter. The result is the same regardless of position.

5 + 3 = 3 + 5
+

Associative

Grouping doesn't matter. Parentheses position won't change the sum.

(2+3)+4 = 2+(3+4)

Distributive

Multiplication spreads across addition inside parentheses.

2×(3+4) = 2×3 + 2×4
