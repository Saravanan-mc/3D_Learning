The Family Tree
of Numbers

Numbers are classified into nested sets. Every natural number is also an integer, and every integer is also a rational number.

Natural Numbers (ℕ) {1, 2, 3...}

Used for counting things. The simplest number set.

Whole Numbers (𝕎) {0, 1, 2...}

Natural numbers plus zero. Introduces the concept of "None".

Integers (ℤ) {...-1, 0, 1...}

Includes whole numbers and their negative opposites.

Rational Numbers (ℚ) {1/2, 0.75}

Any number that can be expressed as a fraction $p/q$.
