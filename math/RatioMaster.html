Introduction to Ratios

What is a Ratio?

A ratio is a comparison of two or more quantities. It tells us how much of one thing there is compared to another. Ratios can be written as a:b, a to b, or a/b.

Ways to write:
3:2 3 to 2

Rates & Unit Rates

The "Unit Rate" Concept

A Rate compares quantities with different units (like miles and hours). A Unit Rate is a rate where the second quantity is exactly 1 (like miles per 1 hour).

Example:

$15 for 3 pizzas

→

$5 per pizza
