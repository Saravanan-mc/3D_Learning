The Core Ratios

SOH CAH TOA

In a right-angled triangle, we use these ratios to find missing sides or angles:

Sine (sin θ) = Opposite / Hypotenuse
Cosine (cos θ) = Adjacent / Hypotenuse
Tangent (tan θ) = Opposite / Adjacent
Sin: 0.50
Cos: 0.87
Tan: 0.58

3D Trigonometry

The Spiral Helix

By plotting $x = \cos(t)$, $y = \sin(t)$, and $z = t$, we create a 3D helix. This demonstrates how periodic circular motion translates into linear progress.
